Fair questions

What a careful owner asks first.

Can't I just use ChatGPT myself?+

You can. Concierge adds structured build sessions and hands-on help with your own workflows. The planned monthly cadence gives you time to use, test and improve them.

Is my data safe?+

Where data goes depends on the tools you choose. Cloud account settings and provider terms apply to Concierge workflows. For local processing, review a Sovereign deployment and its network controls. Screen sharing also needs agreed access and handling rules.

How much of my time does it take?+

The proposed Concierge plan includes ninety minutes a month on calls. Allow time to test and use each workflow between sessions. Time saved varies by task.

What if my staff won't use it?+

They don't have to on day one. We build for you first. When your team sees you clearing reviews in five minutes, they'll ask.

Do you build custom apps?+

Not in this program. We build workflows in tools you already pay for. If you need custom software later, we'll say so plainly.

What are the proposed terms?+

The proposal is month to month for Concierge and a six-month engagement for Premium. Cancellation, ownership and support terms must be agreed before booking.
