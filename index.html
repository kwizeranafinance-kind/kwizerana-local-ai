Fair questions

What a careful partner asks first.

Is our data really never leaving?+

Yes. The model runs on the appliance in your office and needs no outbound connection to answer. Every response is logged with a signed record of zero egress — a claim you can verify, not one you have to believe.

Is a local AI actually good enough?+

For document work — answering, drafting, summarizing across your own files — yes, comfortably. For the rare frontier-level task, we're honest: keep one cloud subscription for the 10% that needs it, and keep the sensitive 90% sealed inside.

How much work is this for my team?+

Almost none. We build, train, and seal it; your team asks it questions in plain English. There's no dashboard to learn and no IT project — the only thing on your desk is the final approval.

What does it cost?+

Setup runs $3,500–5,000, monthly support $3,500–6,000 depending on scope, and the compliance receipt wall $1,500–3,000. The hardware itself is billed through at cost — we make nothing on the box. One retained matter typically covers it.

What if it breaks?+

Deployments run redundant hardware and we monitor health remotely — without any path to the data inside. A failure is an operational event with a runbook, never a loss of your files or your privacy.
