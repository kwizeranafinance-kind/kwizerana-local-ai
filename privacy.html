What this website collects

If you complete the fit check or request a briefing, we collect the contact details you provide (name, firm, email) so we can respond. We use privacy-respecting analytics that do not track you across the web. We do not sell your data, ever.

The Fit Finder

Your answers to the fit check are processed in your browser to show your result. We receive them only if you choose to submit your contact details for a follow-up.
