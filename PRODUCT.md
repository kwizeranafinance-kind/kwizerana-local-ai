# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Business owners exploring hands-on AI support, and law, medical and finance firms evaluating local AI. The existing homepage addresses firms handling sensitive files; the supplied Concierge page addresses everyday business workflows.

## Product Purpose
Explain the Kwizerana AI offering and help visitors choose between guided workflows in existing accounts and an on-premise Sovereign deployment. This is a static marketing website, not the AI runtime.

## Brand Personality
Practical, personal, privacy-conscious. Preserve the identity established by the existing site and the user-supplied Enclave Concierge page.

## Anti-references
Avoid introducing a different visual identity or making a static marketing illustration look like a connected AI application. Do not blur cloud accounts and local processing.

## Design Principles
- Preserve the supplied page and existing site identity.
- Keep the existing Sovereign homepage and fit check accessible.
- Explain where each service processes data.
- Distinguish proposed offers and illustrations from verified availability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Retain responsive layouts and reduced-motion support from the existing code. Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-accessible links and disclosures, visible focus, and mobile navigation.
